ABOUT US

The Barangay Appointment System is an online platform designed to make barangay services easier and more convenient for residents. It allows users to view available services and schedule appointments online.

Our goal is to provide a simple, organized, and accessible way for residents to connect with their barangay and save time when requesting services.

◎

Our Mission

To provide convenient, accessible, and organized barangay services through an online appointment system. We aim to make transactions easier for residents and improve the delivery of barangay services.

♟

Our Goal

To help residents save time and effort by allowing them to schedule appointments online. We also aim to reduce long waiting times and make barangay services more accessible to the community.
